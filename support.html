Getting Started 04 questions
+
How long does installation take? +
Installation of the FMC234 device on a single generator typically takes under one hour. No infrastructure changes are required — the device connects directly to the generator panel and communicates over 4G cellular.
Does it work on any generator brand? +
Yes. GenGuardian is brand-agnostic — it works on any standby generator regardless of manufacturer, model, or age. There is no proprietary hardware requirement.
What do I need to have ready before installation? +
Access to the generator panel and a confirmed service contact for SMS alerts. No WiFi credentials, no network configuration, and no changes to existing building infrastructure are needed.
How do I access the monitoring dashboard? +
Your dashboard is accessible at hosting.genguardian.pt. Login credentials are provided after your first unit is activated. Contact support if you have not received your credentials.
Device & Hardware 04 questions
+
What happens if the building loses power? +
The FMC234 has an internal 1000mAh battery backup and communicates over 4G cellular — completely independent of the building's power supply and WiFi. It continues operating through a blackout, which is exactly when it matters most.
The device shows offline in the dashboard. What should I do? +
First check that the device has power and the indicator light is on. If the unit is powered but showing offline, check cellular signal strength at the installation location. Submit a ticket if the issue persists and include the device ID shown in the dashboard.
How often does the device transmit data? +
The device transmits status updates continuously over cellular. Event-based alerts — such as a generator start, stop, or fault — are transmitted immediately in real time.
Does the device require a SIM card? +
Yes. Each device uses a dedicated SIM included with your subscription. The SIM is pre-configured and active — no setup required on your end.
Alerts & Notifications 03 questions
+
Who receives the SMS alerts? +
Alerts are sent to the service contacts configured in your dashboard. By default this is your service team — not the building owner. You control who receives notifications and can update contacts at any time from the dashboard.
What events trigger an alert? +
Configurable alert conditions include: generator failure to start, unexpected shutdown, low fuel, loss of mains power, generator running beyond expected duration, and device going offline. Alert conditions can be adjusted in your dashboard settings.
I am not receiving alerts. What should I check? +
Verify the contact number is correctly entered in the dashboard and that SMS is not blocked by your carrier. Check that alert conditions are enabled for the relevant events. If everything looks correct and alerts are still not arriving, submit a ticket with your device ID and the expected alert type.
Billing & Account 03 questions
+
How does billing work? +
Billing is a monthly subscription per monitored unit, invoiced in arrears. A one-time device fee applies at activation. Invoices are sent to the billing contact on your account. Contact support to update billing details or request copies of past invoices.
How do I add or remove units from my account? +
Contact support to add new units or deactivate existing ones. Changes to your unit count are reflected in the following billing cycle.
How do I update my account contact or login details? +
Login and contact updates can be requested via the support form. Include your current registered email and the details you want updated. Changes are processed within one business day.
Compliance 02 questions
+
Does GenGuardian help meet RT-SCIE requirements? +
Yes. Artigo 72º of Portaria 1532/2008 (RT-SCIE) mandates verified emergency backup power in hotels, hospitals, and large commercial buildings. GenGuardian provides continuous monitoring and documented event logs that support compliance with this requirement.
Can I export monitoring logs for compliance documentation? +
Yes. Event logs and generator activity reports can be exported from your dashboard. Contact support if you need a specific format or date range for a compliance audit.
